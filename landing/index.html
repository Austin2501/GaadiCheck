Your vehicle details never leave your phone.

GaadiCheck is designed to work without an account or a server holding your data.

  • Registration numbers and dates are stored only on your device.
  • RC and PUC images are read on the phone and discarded.
  • Alerts use anonymous topics like "zone-ghaziabad" — never your number.
  • Location is used once, only if you ask, to pick your district.

Questions

Is GaadiCheck an official government app?

No. GaadiCheck is independent and not affiliated with CAQM, CPCB or any government body. We publish the rules we apply with a link to the official source, and you should always follow the latest order and on-ground instructions.

Where does the data come from?

GRAP stages and rules come from CAQM orders. Official air quality comes from the Central Pollution Control Board via data.gov.in, and forecasts from Open-Meteo using Copernicus data.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The last known stage and your verdicts are cached, and the app tells you when it is showing saved data.

Is there an iPhone version?

Android comes first. The app is built cross-platform, so iOS can follow.
