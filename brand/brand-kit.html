Logo

A car with the all-clear.

The mark is a car seen head-on wearing a marigold check badge — the vehicle and today's verdict in one shape. Its number plate nods to the plate badges used across the app. Indigo carries the brand; marigold is kept for the badge and other "signal" moments.

  • Clear space: the badge's diameter on every side
  • Minimum size: mark 24 px · lock-up 96 px wide
  • Never recolour, rotate, outline or stretch the mark
  • Never pair with government emblems, the national flag, the Ashoka Chakra or state seals

Voice

  • Lead with the answer — "You're good to drive", then why.
  • Plain and calm — "Not allowed", never alarmist, no exclamation marks.
  • Specific — "Fine up to ₹20,000", "Expires 30 Nov".
  • Bilingual — English and natively written Hindi.
  • Independent — we point to the official order; we never pose as one.
